      Specialty Coffee India

      A working map of the country's coffee

      India grows coffee in the hills and drinks it everywhere. The map is the country's real terrain — the green plains give way to the brown wall of the Himalaya in the north, the long ridge of the Western Ghats down the west coast, the Eastern Ghats inland, and the folded hills of the Northeast.

      This is a directory, not a ranking. Inclusion is not certification or a quality score, and it is not a shop. A location usually represents the public base shown by the original source, not every branch or facility.
