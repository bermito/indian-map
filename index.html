    Specialty Coffee India

    A working map of the country's coffee

    India grows coffee in the hills and drinks it everywhere. The map is the country's real terrain — the brown wall in the north is the Himalaya, the long ridge down the west coast is the Western Ghats, and the notch halfway down it is the Palakkad Gap.

    Click any state to travel into it. This is a directory, not a ranking. Inclusion is not certification or a quality score, and it is not a shop. A location usually represents the public base shown by the original source, not every branch or facility.
