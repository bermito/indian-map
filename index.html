      The index

      About

      Specialty Coffee India is a living, independent discovery index. We document and help people find the people, places, origins and ideas shaping the future of specialty coffee across India.

      This is a directory, not a ranking. Inclusion is not certification, endorsement or a quality score. It is not an online shop.

      A location usually represents the public base or headquarters shown by the original source. It may not represent every café branch, roasting location or facility. Information can change.

      Source note

      Roaster records were compiled from Indian Coffee Beans and Kaapi Brewing, then normalized and deduplicated for discovery. Source information can change; please submit corrections or additions for review.
